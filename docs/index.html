A bit about me.

I'm a Unity developer with a foot in two camps. On the games side, I've shipped Hotel Tycoon: Idle Manager, Highway Havoc, Supreme Strike, and Goods Match — mobile games spanning idle tycoon, arcade, action, and match genres.

On the XR side I build Mixed Reality and Virtual Reality prototypes — QR-driven object spawners on smart glasses, voice-controlled AI companions in VR, and the playful interactions that make people grin the first time they put on a headset.

I care about the small details — a satisfying tap, a snappy feedback loop, a UI that doesn't get in the way. End-to-end: concept, scripting, scene work, and shipping the build.

Outside of games and XR I also build ASP.NET Core web APIs and backend services in C# — clean REST endpoints, a tidy data layer, and deployments that don't fall over on Monday.
