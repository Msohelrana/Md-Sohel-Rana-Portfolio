01 About

I'm a Unity developer focused on Mixed Reality and Virtual Reality. I love turning weird ideas into things you can reach out and touch — QR-driven object spawners on smart glasses, voice-controlled AI companions in VR, and prototypes that make people grin the first time they put on a headset.

I care about the small details: a responsive grab, a satisfying snap, a UI that doesn't get in the way. I work end-to-end — concept, scripting, scene work, and shipping a build.
